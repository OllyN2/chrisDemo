How to play

The Fiat Multipla seats six people in two rows of three. Use the clues to work out where today's six passengers sit.

Drag passengers into seats, or tap a passenger and then a seat. Drag a seated passenger onto someone else to swap them, or off the car to unseat them. Tap a clue to cross it off.

"Left" and "right" are as seen sitting in the car, facing forward. The driver sits front-left. "Next to" means side by side in the same row.
