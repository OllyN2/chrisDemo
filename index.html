Ship's orders

The 1992 Italdesign Columbus concept carries its passengers in rows of two behind a raised central driver's seat. Read the captain's log to work out where today's six passengers sit in the front, middle and back rows.

Drag passengers into seats, or tap a passenger and then a seat. Drag a seated passenger onto someone else to swap them, or off the van to send them back to boarding. Tap a log entry to cross it off.

"Left" and "right" are as seen sitting in the van, facing forward. "Directly behind" means one row back, on the same side.
